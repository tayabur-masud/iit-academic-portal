Grades

One grade scale, shared by every degree. Each band runs from its lower bound up to, but not including, its upper bound; the band that ends at 100% includes 100%. The active bands must cover 0% to 100% with no gaps or overlaps. An inactive band is kept but not used. Edit the bands, then save them together.
